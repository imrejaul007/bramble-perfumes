Why We Exist
"NUHOUD was never created to sell perfume.
It was created to preserve one of the world's
greatest forgotten crafts."
The luxury fragrance industry has become increasingly disconnected from the origins of its most precious materials. NUHOUD exists to reconnect people with the complete journey of natural perfumery, from ancient agarwood forests and traditional distillation, to rare oud oils, attars, and perfumes crafted without compromise. Every creation begins with nature, not marketing.
Our Mission
To become the world's most respected house of natural perfumery by preserving heritage, creating extraordinary fragrances, and educating future generations about the true art of oud.
